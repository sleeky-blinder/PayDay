Rules

  1. Money moves on payday and on the 1st. Between those dates, nothing moves.
  2. Flex holds nothing overnight.
  3. No new money into the Equity Portfolio until it is under 25% of the whole.
  4. Speculative sleeve: ₦120,000/mo cap, never topped up after a loss.
  5. Nothing gets locked again.
  6. 48-hour rule: any unplanned move above ₦100,000 is written down and waits two days.
  7. Compare naira to naira and dollars to dollars — never across.
  8. The dollar sleeve stays in two places — PiggyVest and Cowrywise.
